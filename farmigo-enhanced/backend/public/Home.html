About Us

Farmigo was born from a vision to create a more equitable ecosystem for Indian farmers. Despite their critical role in our economy, many farmers still face challenges in accessing fair markets and digital tools.

Built with modern web technologies, Farmigo is more than just an online store. It is a community-driven initiative focused on rural upliftment, traceability of produce, and promoting ethical food consumption.
